Specialized industry: hubs, vehicles, field props

Geometry is dumped from the engine (industry_dump in src/city_industry.inl), so these are the in-game meshes. Walls and flat roofs are SPOM slabs: in-game their relief (pilasters, recessed windows, doors, ribs) is ray-marched from the texture's height channel; here they show the albedo only. Triangle counts include every slab face (10 per face).
